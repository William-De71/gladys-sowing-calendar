## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- A sowing calendar for the kitchen garden, computed locally: when to sow under cover, sow outdoors, plant out and harvest 55 crops (43 vegetables, 12 herbs), to tick in the configuration.
- Dates for the climate of the house: five climate zones that move the spring and the autumn, a fine adjustment in weeks and the southern hemisphere.
- A "Sowing calendar" Gladys calendar for every user who enables the integration in "My calendars": one all-day event per crop and task over its whole window, a year ahead, in the language of each user; the tasks shown can be chosen.
- Optional favourable lunar days (Maria Thun method): the days of the type of each crop, days to avoid excluded, in the calendar, the widgets and the scenes.
- Scene triggers "Daily summary", "A window starts" and "Last week of a window", at a chosen hour, with filters on the tasks, the crops and the lunar day type.
- Scene action "Get the garden tasks" (today or tomorrow), with a ready-made sentence.
- Dashboard widgets "In the garden" (the tasks in progress, what starts within the week, what ends soon) and "Crop" (the dates of one crop).
- English, French, German and Spanish everywhere: settings, calendar, widgets, scenes and documentation.
